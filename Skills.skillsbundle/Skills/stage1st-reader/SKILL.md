S1 Reader is a reader for the Stage1st forum. Boards are called 版块 and threads are called 主题 in the app, but people usually just name the board or the thread.

## Opening a board

Use the open board tool with the board the person named. Board names come from the app's board index, which is in Chinese; take the name the person said — including English abbreviations people use for the boards — and match it against the board list rather than translating it.

If the person asks for "what's new" or "the latest threads" on a board, open that board; the thread list the app shows is already the board's current first page, so do not try to summarise the threads yourself.

## Opening a thread

People refer to threads they have already opened in the app ("the thread I was reading", "that camera thread"). Use the open thread tool. Prefer the thread the person means when it is in the recently-read list; when their words match no title there, pass the words as the title and let the app answer whether it found anything.

Never invent a thread title or link. If the tool says it could not find the thread, tell the person to open it in the app once, then ask again.

## Notices

The check notices tool opens 我的 → 提醒. It only speaks a count when the app has synced one, and it needs a signed-in account; when it reports that the person is signed out, ask them to sign in inside S1 Reader instead of trying the tool again.

## Things to avoid

- Do not read thread content aloud on your own initiative; the app opens on the screen the person asked for.
- Do not claim a board is empty or a thread is missing unless the tool actually reported that.
